§ 50a WPO — *Inanspruchnahme von Dienstleistungen*. The auditors' counterpart to § 62a StBerG and § 43e BRAO, from the same 2017 act (BT-Drs. 18/11936). Abs. 1 refers the confidentiality duty to § 43 WPO; the remaining structure matches the sibling provisions: careful selection and termination duty (Abs. 2), Textform contract with criminal-consequence instruction, need-to-know limitation and sub-processor clause (Abs. 3), comparable-protection test for services performed abroad (Abs. 4), client consent for single-mandate services (Abs. 5), and data-protection law expressly unaffected.

**Why archived (2026-08-16, third-pass thesis review).** Supplies the correct authority for the *Wirtschaftsprüfer* branch of the Chapter 1 legal argument, which had been sourced to the tax-advisor statute. Cited in `thesis/references.bib` as `wpo50a`.
