§ 33 UStDV — small-amount invoices (Kleinbetragsrechnungen): invoices whose gross
total does not exceed 250 EUR may omit several of the § 14 UStG particulars
(recipient identity, sequential number, separate tax amount), needing only issuer,
issue date, supply description, gross amount and tax rate. Cited in thesis ch. 2 as
the reason field *presence* varies legitimately across real invoices — a receipt-like
Beleg lawfully omits fields a full invoice must carry — which is one root of the
honest-null contract: an absent field is frequently correct, not a defect.
