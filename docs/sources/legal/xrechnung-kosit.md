XRechnung — the German national CIUS (Core Invoice Usage Specification) of
EN 16931, maintained by KoSIT on behalf of the IT-Planungsrat. Pure-XML electronic
invoice (UBL or UN/CEFACT CII syntax), mandatory for invoices to German public-sector
buyers. Cited in thesis ch. 2 to distinguish the two German EN 16931 carrier forms:
XRechnung (XML-only, machine-first) versus ZUGFeRD/Factur-X (hybrid PDF/A-3 with
embedded CII XML, human-visual page plus machine layer in one file). The hybrid form
is what makes the thesis's evaluation loop possible — the same document carries the
rendered page (system input) and the structured reference (ground truth).
