§ 43e BRAO — *Inanspruchnahme von Dienstleistungen*. The lawyers' counterpart to § 62a StBerG, introduced by the same 2017 act (BT-Drs. 18/11936) that added § 203 Abs. 3–4 StGB. Structurally near-identical: Abs. 1 permits opening access to facts covered by the confidentiality duty of § 43a Abs. 2 Satz 1 BRAO where necessary for the service; Abs. 2 requires careful selection and immediate termination if the Abs. 3 requirements are not met; Abs. 3 requires a contract **in Textform** obliging the provider to secrecy **with instruction about the criminal consequences**, restricting it to need-to-know, and settling whether sub-processors may be used (if so, they must be bound in Textform too); Abs. 4 requires **comparable secrecy protection** for services performed abroad; Abs. 5 requires **client consent** where the service serves one individual mandate; Abs. 8 leaves data-protection law untouched.

**Why archived (2026-08-16, third-pass thesis review).** Thesis Chapter 1 named tax advisors, auditors *and* lawyers, then sourced the service-provider requirements to § 62a StBerG alone — a statute that covers only Steuerberater and Steuerbevollmächtigte. This record supplies the correct authority for the lawyer branch. Cited in `thesis/references.bib` as `brao43e`.
