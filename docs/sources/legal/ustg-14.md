§ 14 UStG — the statutory basis for German invoices. Abs. 4 lists the mandatory
particulars (Pflichtangaben) an invoice must carry: full names/addresses of both
parties, the supplier's tax number or USt-IdNr., issue date, sequential invoice
number, quantity and description of supply, date of supply, net amount broken down
by rate, applicable tax rate and tax amount, and reduction agreements. Since the
Wachstumschancengesetz amendments, § 14 also defines the *elektronische Rechnung*
(structured electronic invoice per EN 16931) as the default B2B form, with a
phased-in obligation (receipt capability since 2025; issuance obligations staged
thereafter). Cited in thesis ch. 2 as the legal grounding for the EN 16931 field
registry: the BT-numbered business terms the registry aligns to cover these
particulars, which is what makes the extraction target "legally meaningful" rather
than an arbitrary schema.
