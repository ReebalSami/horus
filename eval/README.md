# eval/

Committed evaluation **evidence** for HORUS: audit reports and sanitized
aggregate results the thesis cites. The evaluation *code* lives in
`src/horus/eval/` (scorer, adapters, ground truth, held-out route); the
*runners* live in `scripts/` and the `Makefile`.

## Tracking status

`eval/` is **tracked**. Everything here is small, text-based, and either
hand-authored analysis or a machine-generated report whose regeneration
command is named in its header. Nothing here contains private held-out
invoice content — held-out results are committed as **aggregates only**
(counts + pooled scores; the pattern of `docs/architecture/belege-heldout-datasheet.md`).

Related but stdout-only (nothing written here): `make audit-heldout-exclusions`
re-derives the ADR-072 per-cell EXCLUDED-cause counts from the private corpus
(LOCAL-ONLY — requires the restored held-out set).

## Conventions

- **Generated files say so** in their first lines and name their regeneration
  command; never hand-edit them.
- **Aggregates only** for anything derived from the private held-out set —
  no field values, no filenames, no invoice ids beyond the sanitized
  `belege-*` scheme.
- Large corpora and model outputs belong under `data/` (gitignored), not here.
