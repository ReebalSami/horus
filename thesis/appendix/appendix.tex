\chapter{AI-Tool Usage Documentation}
\label{app:ai-usage}

In accordance with the FH Wedel thesis guidelines and the statutory declaration,
this appendix documents the use of artificial-intelligence tools in producing
this thesis.

% TODO: complete with specifics (tools, tasks, extent, verification practice).
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Tools used:} large-language-model assistants (e.g.\ Cascade/Claude,
        GitHub~Copilot) for code scaffolding, refactoring, drafting support, and
        documentation.
  \item \textbf{Where used:} implementation of the experimental harness and
        evaluation code; drafting and editing of prose; literature triage.
  \item \textbf{Verification:} all AI-assisted output was reviewed by the author;
        experimental results are reproducible from the repository and were not
        generated by AI. Decision provenance is recorded as Architecture Decision
        Records (see Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).
\end{itemize}
