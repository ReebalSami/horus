This thesis investigates how well current open-source, locally hosted document
Vision-Language Models (VLMs) handle the German business-to-business accounting
workflow end to end, under the legally binding confidentiality constraints
(\S203~StGB, \S62a~StBerG) that prevent tax advisors from sending client
documents to cloud services. The system is structured in three layers ---
document extraction, automatic knowledge-graph (KG) construction, and analytical
querying --- and is evaluated with a compliance-aware metric suite against
EU-hosted cloud baselines.
