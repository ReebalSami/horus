German tax advisors, auditors and lawyers process client invoices under a
professional-secrecy regime backed by criminal law, which makes sending those
documents to external cloud services legally fraught and motivates processing
that never leaves the company. This thesis asks how reliably open-weights
vision-language models sized for hardware a company already owns --- nothing
reported required more than a single 24\,GB graphics processor, and the
structuring stage runs on a 16\,GB laptop --- can convert German
business-to-business invoices into the legally
grounded \acs{EN}~16931 semantic form, and which stage of the pipeline limits
that reliability. It builds and measures HORUS, a two-stage pipeline in which
Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct transcribes the page image and gemma-4-E4B-it structures
the transcript against the field registry; no inference path makes a network
call. Measurement
rests on a synthetic \acf{ZUGFeRD} corpus whose embedded \acs{XML} supplies
machine-exact references, and on 39 real invoices with an answer key adjudicated
across three independent channels.

Zero-shot header-field extraction on the real invoices reaches a mean
per-invoice \acs{F1} of 0.88, with precision (0.95) well above recall (0.85).
Capture quality, not language, is the dominant cost --- phone-scanned documents
score more than eleven points below email-native ones. Because the structuring
stage exceeds 0.97 on perfect transcripts, the binding constraint is reading,
confirmed by per-miss attribution of every error. A pre-registered low-rank
adaptation of the structuring stage regresses in all four cells of its
two-by-two design (training input distribution crossed with evaluation
condition) and specifically raises the rate of values invented for absent
fields.

A knowledge-graph layer and a query layer were designed but deliberately not
built or evaluated; their hypotheses are reported as registered but unevaluated.
A recurring finding is methodological: the measuring apparatus itself required
validation. Documented defects in scoring rules and reference data moved
reported figures by amounts comparable to between-model differences and once
inverted a model ordering; the thesis contributes the defect taxonomy, the
frozen-generation re-scoring discipline that makes repairs measurable in
isolation, and the corrected results. For deployment the error profile
matters as much as the score: four errors in five are omissions, so failures
predominantly surface as blank fields a reviewer can look up, not as
plausible-looking wrong values --- a failure mode compatible with
human-in-the-loop verification on hardware the company already owns.
