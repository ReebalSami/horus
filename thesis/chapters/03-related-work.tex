\chapter{Related Work}
\label{ch:related-work}

This chapter positions the thesis against four bodies of work: vision-based document
information extraction, the evaluation of extraction quality, parameter-efficient
adaptation of compact models, and knowledge-graph construction. The first three each end
with a statement of what this thesis adds. The fourth deliberately does not, because the
layers it informed were designed but not built (\S\ref{sec:design-scope}), and honesty
about that is worth more than a claimed contribution.

\section{Vision-Based Invoice and Document Information Extraction}
\label{sec:rw-extraction}

\subsection{From templates to document vision-language models}

Invoice extraction is an old industrial problem, and its method history explains the model
class this thesis evaluates. The first generation paired \ac{OCR} with templates and rules:
reliable on layouts it had seen, expensive on layouts it had not, and maintained by hand as
suppliers changed their stationery. The second generation replaced the rules with learned
models over the \ac{OCR} output, most prominently the LayoutLM line, whose third iteration
pre-trains a single transformer over text, layout and image patches with unified masking
objectives \parencite{huang2022layoutlmv3}. These models still consume an external \ac{OCR}
engine's output, so their extraction quality is bounded by a reading stage they do not
control --- an architectural echo of the attribution question this thesis asks. The third
generation removed the \ac{OCR} dependency altogether: Donut demonstrated that an
encoder--decoder trained end-to-end can map a page image directly to a structured output
sequence with no intermediate text at all \parencite{kim2022donut}.

The fourth generation --- the subject of this thesis --- inverts Donut's bet. Rather than
one end-to-end model per task, it offers general document \emph{transcription} as a
foundation capability, packaged in small open-weights models explicitly built for local
deployment. Within roughly a year of this writing there appeared, among others,
granite-docling-258M, a compact end-to-end conversion model \parencite{granitedocling}
underpinning the Docling toolchain \parencite{docling2025}; olmOCR-2, a seven-billion-parameter
high-throughput transcription model \parencite{olmocr2}; the MinerU~2.5 parsing family
\parencite{wang2026mineru}; the DeepSeek-OCR line, which frames page reading as optical context
compression \parencite{deepseekocr2025,deepseekocr2026}; and general-purpose compact \acp{VLM}
with strong document ability, such as Qwen3-VL \parencite{qwen3vl}. This wave is recent enough
that the survey and selection in Chapters~\ref{ch:measurement-validity}
and~\ref{ch:results} constitute, to the author's knowledge, the first systematic comparison
of its members on German business-to-business invoices.

\subsection{Benchmarks and corpora}

The standard public corpora for invoice-adjacent extraction do not cover this thesis's
setting. FUNSD targets form understanding in noisy scans \parencite{jaume2019funsd};
CORD-v2 covers Indonesian till receipts \parencite{cordv2}; FATURA2 provides synthetic invoices
from fifty templates without a legally grounded schema \parencite{fatura2}. A corpus of 977 real
annotated German invoices exists but is access-restricted and could not be used here
\parencite{krieger2021german}. On the benchmark side, OmniDocBench measures document parsing
broadly \parencite{omnidocbench}, Real5-OmniDocBench extends it with five physical capture
conditions \parencite{real5omnidocbench}, and MDPBench measures multilingual parsing across
3{,}400 documents in 17 languages with a private evaluation split, reporting that
open-source parsers lose an average of 17.8\,\% on photographed relative to digital
documents \parencite{mdpbench2026}. None of these pairs German \ac{B2B} invoices with a
machine-exact semantic reference. The \ac{ZUGFeRD} corpus this thesis builds on
(\S\ref{sec:method-data}) does exactly that: each document embeds its own reference as
structured data under the \ac{EN}~16931 semantic model \parencite{en16931}, which is what makes
scoring exact and ground truth free --- at the price of visual cleanliness, a price
Chapter~\ref{ch:results} measures.

\subsection{The closest prior work, and where its finding points the other way}
\label{sec:rw-berghaus}

The nearest neighbour to this thesis is the invoice-processing benchmark of
\textcite{berghaus2025}. They evaluate eight multi-modal models from three families ---
cloud-frontier models and open-weights general-purpose Gemma~3 --- on three public invoice
and receipt datasets, under two processing strategies: native image input, and a
parse-first arrangement that converts the document to markdown with Docling
\parencite{docling2025} before a text-only model extracts the fields. Their headline finding is
that \textbf{native image processing generally outperforms the parse-first arrangement}.

Taken at face value, that finding points in the opposite direction from this thesis's
two-stage design, and the tension deserves to be stated rather than avoided. Three
differences keep the results compatible. First, the model classes barely overlap: their
strongest results come from cloud-frontier models far above this thesis's local envelope,
and their open-weights models are general-purpose --- the specialised document
\acp{VLM} that constitute this thesis's reading candidates are absent. Second, the
parse-first arrangements differ in kind: their text arm is a layout-analysis converter
producing markdown, whereas this thesis's reading stage is itself a \ac{VLM} transcriber,
selected competitively (\S\ref{sec:results-reader}) --- ``parse-first loses with one
converter'' does not establish that every two-stage arrangement loses. Third, and most
importantly, this thesis's decisive argument for the split is not accuracy but
\emph{measurability}: a stored transcript is what makes per-stage attribution and
instrument validation possible at all (\S\ref{sec:design-two-stage}), and those
measurements, not the end-to-end score, produced this thesis's central findings. The
accuracy evidence gathered here --- a small preliminary comparison in which the two-stage
arrangement won (\S\ref{sec:results-preliminary}), consistent with published evidence that
imposed output formats degrade generation quality \parencite{tam2024format} --- is weaker than
their benchmark and is labelled as such. Whether a single-shot arrangement overtakes the
split \emph{within} the specialised local model class is an open question this thesis
leaves explicitly to future work (\S\ref{sec:future-work}).

\textbf{What this thesis adds} to this line of work: the missing model class (specialised
open-weights document \acp{VLM} under an enforced local-hardware envelope), the missing
corpus class (German \ac{B2B} invoices with a legally grounded, machine-exact reference,
plus 39 real invoices with an adjudicated answer key), and a per-stage attribution that
benchmark-style end-to-end scores cannot provide.

\section{Evaluation of Extraction Quality and Measurement Validity}
\label{sec:rw-evaluation}

\subsection{Matching rules for extraction scoring}

How a predicted value is compared against a reference is a design decision with measurable
consequences, and the field has revised it repeatedly. Exact string match penalises
harmless representation differences, which motivated \ac{ANLS}, a normalised edit-distance
tolerance originally introduced for scene-text question answering \parencite{biten2019anls}.
ANLS$^{\star}$ generalised that tolerance to the structured outputs of document extraction
--- lists, nested objects, absent fields \parencite{peer2024anlsstar}. KIEval moved the
evaluation closer to industrial use by scoring grouped entities (a line item's fields
belonging together) separately from individual ones, arguing that extraction quality in
application settings is experienced as correction cost, not as isolated cell accuracy
\parencite{kieval2025}. This thesis's metric suite (\S\ref{sec:method-metrics}) sits in that
lineage: field-level scoring under symmetric, type-aware canonicalisation, group-level
cells reported alongside flat ones, and abstention scored as a first-class outcome ---
the last being a property none of the cited rules make explicit, and which the honest-null
contract of this thesis requires.

\subsection{Measurement validity as a first-class concern}

A separate line of work holds that aggregate scores conceal more than they reveal.
CheckList transferred behavioural testing from software engineering to \ac{NLP} models,
showing that models with identical accuracy fail in categorically different ways
\parencite{ribeiro2020checklist}. \ac{HELM} argued for multi-metric, transparently scoped
evaluation of language models \parencite{liang2022helm}. Community instruments such as the
NeurIPS reproducibility checklist \parencite{neuripschecklist} and engineering guidance such as
Zinkevich's rules \parencite{zinkevich2018rules} institutionalise the same concern at the
process level, and the pre-registration discipline this thesis follows descends from the
methodological literature on \ac{HARKing} \parencite{kerr1998}. Datasheets for datasets extend
the concern to the data itself \parencite{gebru2018datasheets}, and this thesis records its
held-out corpus in that form (Appendix~\ref{app:heldout}).

What is largely missing from the extraction literature is the step this thesis takes in
Chapter~\ref{ch:measurement-validity}: treating the \emph{scoring apparatus itself} as an
instrument under test. The benchmark papers above report scores; they do not report the
defect history of their own comparison rules, and published extraction results rarely
state how sensitive their rankings are to representation handling. This thesis documents
that sensitivity on its own data --- scoring corrections moved reported figures by amounts
comparable to the differences between models, and the ordering of two candidate readers
inverted under a ruler repair (\S\ref{sec:results-reader}) --- which suggests the
sensitivity is not a peculiarity of this project.

\textbf{What this thesis adds}: a documented defect taxonomy for extraction scoring on a
task where the reference is machine-readable while the input is a rendered page; the
discipline of re-scoring frozen generations so that instrument repairs are measurable in
isolation from model changes; and an attribution design that bounds the structuring
stage's contribution using reference-quality transcripts
(\S\ref{sec:method-attribution}).

\section{Parameter-Efficient Adaptation of Compact Models}
\label{sec:rw-adaptation}

Low-rank adaptation trains small additive matrices while freezing the base weights, and is
the standard method for adapting a model of this size on rented hardware \parencite{hu2021lora}.
A growing body of evidence, however, bounds what such adaptation can add and warns what it
can break. \textcite{ghosh2024limitations} find that \ac{LoRA}-based instruction tuning
``is limited to learning response initiation and style tokens'', with most of the answer
content drawn from pre-trained knowledge. \textcite{kang2024unfamiliar} show that how a
fine-tuned model behaves under uncertainty is controlled by the unfamiliar examples in its
training set. \textcite{uluoglakci2026humility} diagnose that
standard supervised fine-tuning ``implicitly rewards always responding'' --- across 800
controlled \ac{LoRA} runs, including on the predecessor generation of this thesis's
structuring model --- and treat abstention as a skill that must be explicitly taught.
\textcite{kaplan2026why} locate fine-tuning-induced hallucination in
interference with pre-trained knowledge and show that freezing parameter groups can
preserve task performance while reducing it.

This literature predicts the shape, though not the certainty, of the negative result in
\S\ref{sec:results-adaptation}: an adapter trained on a corpus whose supervision mostly
rewards emitting values learns, among other things, to emit --- and this thesis's task
contractually requires the opposite on absent fields. Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} develops
the mechanism.

\textbf{What this thesis adds}: a pre-registered, four-cell negative result under a
matched-precision baseline, on a task with a contractual abstention requirement --- a
setting in which the always-respond gradient of standard fine-tuning is directly measurable
as a first-class metric (the spurious-emission rate) rather than as an impression.

\section{Knowledge-Graph Construction and Graph-Augmented Retrieval}
\label{sec:rw-kg}

Three works shaped the design of the layers this thesis specified but did not build
(\S\ref{sec:design-layer2}, \S\ref{sec:design-layer3}). \textcite{edge2024graphrag}
introduced GraphRAG, retrieval over an
extracted graph rather than flat chunks, for query-focused summarisation over corpora;
\textcite{han2025} survey the graph-versus-flat retrieval question
systematically. Most relevantly to the evaluation this thesis would have
needed, \textcite{cai2025} evaluate knowledge-graph extraction at two levels simultaneously
--- edge-level accuracy in the standard \ac{NLP} sense, and the bias that extraction error
induces in downstream graph-level analyses --- and find that below a quality threshold,
graph metrics drift in consistent, metric-specific directions. That
two-level framing is the direct precedent for the fidelity metrics the unbuilt Layer~2
registered in its hypothesis set.

No contribution to this literature is claimed. The registered-but-unevaluated hypotheses
that would have engaged it are reported in Chapter~\ref{ch:limitations-future-work}.

\section{Positioning of This Thesis}
\label{sec:rw-positioning}

The gap this thesis fills sits at the intersection of the sections above. The specialised
open-weights document \acp{VLM} of 2025--26 exist precisely to make local document
understanding practical, yet the closest invoice benchmark predates them and evaluates
cloud-frontier and general-purpose models instead \parencite{berghaus2025}; the public corpora
do not pair German \ac{B2B} invoices with a machine-exact legal schema; and the evaluation
literature supplies matching rules but not the discipline of validating the scoring
apparatus that applies them. This thesis occupies that intersection: it evaluates the new
model class on the corpus class it will actually meet in a German firm, under a
privacy-motivated hardware envelope stated in advance, with an attribution method that
locates the binding constraint (reading, not structuring), and with the measuring
instrument itself validated, repaired and reported alongside the system it measures.
Where the closest prior work and this thesis point in different architectural directions
--- native image input against a two-stage split --- \S\ref{sec:rw-berghaus} argues the
results are compatible once model class, converter class and the measurability argument
are separated, and the residual disagreement is stated as an open question rather than
resolved by assertion.
